Bogey Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Bogey is a mini golf game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Questions can go to bogeygame1@gmail.com.

What we collect

Bogey has no accounts and does not ask you for your name, email address or any other personal information.

Your coins, power-ups, unlocked courses, progress, scorecards and settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and leave "Sync with iCloud" on in the app's Settings, a copy of your progress is kept in your own iCloud account through Apple so it follows you to your other devices. We cannot see it. You can turn sync off in Settings.

Crash reports and usage statistics

Bogey uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to find bugs and understand how the game is played. They collect:

  • Crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, the device model and iOS version.
  • Gameplay events: for example when a game starts or ends, how a hole went, which items are bought with in-game coins, and when an ad is shown or watched.
  • Technical information: an app instance ID, app version, device type, country (estimated from the IP address) and, only if you allow tracking, your advertising ID.

This information is not linked to your name or contact details, and we do not sell it. Learn more in Firebase's privacy information.

Ads

Bogey shows full-screen ads using Google AdMob. Ads appear only between holes, never during play. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use device identifiers such as your advertising ID, your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Tracking permission: on iPhone, the app asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can say no and still play everything. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the EU, UK and similar regions: where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads. You can reopen your choices any time from Settings > Privacy Choices inside the app.
  • Optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch an ad to keep going when you run out of hearts, or for free in-game coins in the shop. These only play when you tap to watch one.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase that turns off all automatic ads. The optional rewarded ads stay available unless you also switch off "Watch-ad offers" in Settings.

Purchases

Purchases, including Remove Ads, are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

Course downloads

The app downloads public course, theme and event data from Google Firebase so new content can arrive without an app update. These requests contain no personal information, but like any internet request they reveal your IP address to the server.

To make sure these requests come from a genuine copy of Bogey, the app uses Firebase App Check with Apple's App Attest. This sends a short-lived token generated by your device. It contains no personal information.

Notifications

Bogey can send you notifications about new courses, events and other news, but only if you turn on "New courses & events" in the app's Settings (or say yes when the app asks you once). You can turn them off any time in Bogey's Settings or in your iPhone's Settings app. The daily challenge reminder is a separate setting; it is created on your phone and is not sent from a server.

To deliver notifications, Bogey uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service. When you turn notifications on, your device gets a notification token and a Firebase installation ID, which are used only to deliver Bogey's messages. They are not linked to your name, email address or Game Center account, and are not used for advertising. Turning notifications off stops all messages to your device.

Gift codes

Gift codes are checked on your device against a list the app downloads. Codes you have used are saved with your progress (and in iCloud if sync is on) so each code can only be used once. Nothing about redeeming a code is sent to us.

Game Center

Multiplayer and leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center name, friends, invites, match data and leaderboard scores under Apple's Privacy Policy. During a multiplayer game, your ball's position, scores and Game Center name are shared with the other players in that game. We do not receive or store this information.

Children

Bogey is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking or change your consent as described above.
  • Turn off "Sync with iCloud", or use "Reset All Progress" in Settings to erase your progress on your device and in iCloud.
  • Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.
  • Contact us at bogeygame1@gmail.com with any privacy request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
